ConcertTix

Concert Ticket Booking & Discovery Platform

ConcertTix is a web-based concert ticket discovery and booking platform designed to make it easier for users to explore concerts, compare ticket facilities across tiers, and manage ticket purchases through a unified, responsive interface.

Problem & Solution

Traditional concert ticket acquisition often suffered from opaque ticket benefit comparisons, physical queue bottlenecks, and fragmented booking confirmations.

Documented Challenges
  • Physical queues during ticket purchases causing friction and delays for concertgoers.
  • Difficulty understanding differences between various ticket facilities and tiers.
  • Lack of transparent, side-by-side facility comparison for prospective buyers.
  • Need for accurate real-time ticket quota management per ticket class.
Implemented Solution
  • Centralized web catalog enabling fast concert discovery and multifaceted filtering.
  • Three explicit ticket categories: Regular, VIP, and Platinum with transparent facility matrices.
  • End-to-end purchasing workflow from concert selection, tier choice, checkout, to order confirmation.
  • Dedicated user profile portal displaying booking history and digital ticket verification.
